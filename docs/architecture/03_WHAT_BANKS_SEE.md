# What Banks See: Institutional Credit & Underwriting Portal

## 1. Overview & Institutional Mandate

The institutional banking workspace is mounted at `/bank/*` (specifically `/bank/dashboard`). It is engineered for agricultural loan officers, credit underwriters, and risk managers from scheduled commercial banks, regional rural banks (RRBs), and district central cooperative banks (DCCBs) (such as MSCB Baramati branch).

```
/bank
 └── /dashboard      -> Credit Underwriting Queue, Dossier Review & Consent Audit
```

The portal provides an empirical, data-backed underwriting cockpit that replaces traditional subjective inspections with verifiable soil, satellite, and crop-cycle data.

---

## 2. Institutional Header & Branch Identification

When a bank officer logs in (via Clerk authenticated session with `BANK` role), the dashboard displays:
- **Financial Institution Banner**: e.g., *Maharashtra State Cooperative Bank (MSCB)* - *Baramati Agri Credit Division*.
- **Officer Credentials**: Officer Name, Employee ID, Branch IFSC Code (`MSCB0002104`), and RBI regulatory compliance license tags.
- **Credit Portfolio Metrics**:
  - **Active Portfolio**: Total volume of disbursed agricultural credit.
  - **Pending Applications**: Number of dossiers awaiting credit decisions.
  - **Average CIBIL / Mazhi Credit Score**: Composite credit-worthiness metric.
  - **Repayment Recovery Rate**: Real-time recovery percentage of seasonal crop loans.

## 3. Credit Underwriting Pipeline & Queue

The dashboard organizes incoming loan requests into four distinct operational tabs:
1. **Pending Verification** (`SUBMITTED`): Fresh applications requiring document integrity checks.
2. **Under Inspection** (`UNDER_REVIEW`): Active cases where agronomic indicators and land extracts are being inspected.
3. **Approved Pipeline** (`APPROVED`): Sanctioned loans pending escrow release or Kisan Credit Card ledger credit.
4. **Historical Archive** (`REJECTED` / `DISBURSED`): Closed cases retained for statutory RBI audit compliance.

## 4. Farmer Credit Dossier (Inspection Modal)

Clicking **"Inspect Dossier"** loads the comprehensive credit assessment drawer:

### A. Statutory Consent Verification Badge
- Displays the cryptographic consent token generated by the farmer:
  - *"Statutory Consent Granted under Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023"*.
  - Token Hash: `cst_9a87f2b1c4e6` (Signed via Aadhaar OTP).
  - Scope: `CROP_YIELD_HISTORY`, `SOIL_HEALTH_CARD`, `IOT_IRRIGATION_LOGS`.
  - Expiry timestamp: Count down indicating when access automatically terminates.

### B. Masked Identity & Land Records
- **Farmer Name & Contact**: Unmasked under active consent.
- **Aadhaar Number**: Strict masking enforced: `XXXX-XXXX-4819`.
- **7/12 Land Record (Saat Bara)**: Cadastral survey number, verified unencumbered land area (e.g., 6.4 Acres, Gat No. 142/1).
- **Existing Encumbrance Status**: Cross-checked against state revenue land records to prevent double mortgaging.

### C. Agronomic Risk Intelligence (The "Green CIBIL")
Instead of relying solely on past banking history, underwriters view empirical agronomic stability:
- **Historical Crop Yield Stability**: Track record of harvest output over the past 6 seasons (Cotton, Sugarcane, Soybean).
- **Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) Index**: Soil health stability score (indicates resilience against crop failure).
- **IoT Irrigation Integrity**: Confirms whether the farm has active automated drip irrigation or relies entirely on rainfed vulnerability.
- **Climate Risk Exposure**: IMD microclimate flood and drought susceptibility ratings.

### D. Underwriting Actions
The bank officer can execute three terminal actions directly from the drawer:
1. **Approve Credit Limit**: Enter sanctioned amount, interest subvention tier (e.g., $7\%$ interest with $3\%$ prompt repayment incentive under Govt. scheme), and issue digital Sanction Letter.
2. **Request Field Inspection**: Route case to an accredited local agronomist or field surveyor for spot physical verification.
3. **Reject Application**: Record mandatory regulatory justification (e.g., *Insufficient unencumbered land*, *Critical soil degradation risk*, *Unresolved default in CIBIL record*).

## 5. Audit & Compliance Safeguards

- **Access Logging**: Every time an underwriter views or exports a farmer dossier, an unalterable record is committed to `AuditLog`:
  ```json
  {
    "actorId": "usr_bank_officer_mscb_01",
    "action": "VIEW_CREDIT_DOSSIER",
    "targetFarmerId": "farmer_tukaram_481",
    "consentToken": "cst_9a87f2b1c4e6",
    "ipAddress": "103.21.58.12",
    "timestamp": "2026-09-05T12:00:00Z"
  }
  ```
- **Automated Revocation**: Once consent expires or the loan decision is finalized, the officer's decrypt key for sensitive PII is automatically invalidated.
